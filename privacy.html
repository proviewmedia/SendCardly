Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

The short version

  • Sendr is free. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no ads.
  • Your card lives inside its link. We have no database, and we don't keep a copy of any card, message, name or photo.
  • Photos you add stay on your device. They are never uploaded.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits to our own pages, like the home page and the card list. It never runs on card links, so what you write never reaches Google.
  • We don't sell or share data about you, and we don't send marketing emails.

Who we are

Sendr (sendrcards.com) is a Proview Studio project. Questions about this policy go to proviewmediaco@gmail.com.

Your cards

When you make a card, everything about it (the design, the words on the front, your message, the names, the envelope and any stickers) is packed into the link itself. Sendr runs entirely in your browser and has no server that receives or stores cards. When someone opens the link, their browser unpacks the card from it.

This means anyone who has the link can open the card. The link is encoded, not encrypted, so treat it like a letter: send it to the person it's for, and don't put anything in a card you wouldn't write on paper.

When you send a card by text, email or another app, that service carries the link under its own privacy policy.

Photos

Photos you add to a photo card stay on your device. They are not uploaded and they are not included in the link, so the person you send it to sees the card with an empty photo frame.

What's saved on your device

To make the site work, your browser keeps a few things in its own storage on your device. They are never sent to us:

  • The card you're working on, so you don't lose it if you close the page.
  • Who you're replying to, while you pick a card to send back.
  • Whether you've closed the "add to home screen" banner.

Clearing your browser's data for sendrcards.com removes all of it.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit and which pages they use, so we can improve the site. On our pages it collects things like the pages viewed, the type of device and browser, the referring site, and an approximate location that Google works out from your IP address. Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga) to tell visits apart. We see totals in reports, not anyone's identity.

Google Analytics is not loaded when you open a card link, so the words and names inside a card are never sent to Google.

You can block it with your browser's cookie or tracker settings, or with Google's opt-out add-on. Google's handling of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Other services the site uses

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google, which receives your IP address to do so.
  • Pexels hosts the stock images used on some photo cards. Your browser loads them from Pexels.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub may log IP addresses to keep the service running and secure.

Children

Sendr isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Because we don't store cards or accounts, there's nothing about you on our side to look up or delete. You can clear what's saved on your device at any time, and block analytics as described above. If you have a question or request, email proviewmediaco@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
